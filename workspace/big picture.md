## Purpose

This document outlines the team's approach to continuous, well-rounded learning. Our goal is not to become deep specialists in a single area at the expense of everything else, but to maintain a working, up-to-date understanding across all branches relevant to our work. The target is **20% proficiency in each branch** — enough to understand the fundamentals, speak the language, contribute meaningfully, and collaborate effectively with specialists in that area.

Each partition below lists one core resource to study. Links should be added as they are identified and agreed upon by the team.

## Notes

- Each branch should have exactly one primary resource added initially. Additional resources can be proposed and added as the team identifies gaps.
- The 20% target is a floor, not a ceiling. Team members are encouraged to go deeper in their area of specialization while maintaining the baseline across all branches.
- This document should be reviewed and updated periodically as resources are completed and new ones are identified.
